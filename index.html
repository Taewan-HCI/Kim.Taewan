Research

Designing the interaction layer between people and AI, across clinical care, mental health, and multimodal media.

Interaction Design for Multimodal Video AI

Video holds time, space, people, and narrative at once — a chat box isn't the right interface for it. I'm designing interaction languages where direct manipulation (selecting regions, brushing timelines, clicking entities) translates into structured intent that drives AI video reasoning.

In Progress · TwelveLabs Multimodal Interaction Generative UI

LLMs in Clinical & Mental Health Settings

Exploring how LLMs can reduce the gap between clinical information and the people who need it — from AI-assisted journaling that supports psychiatric patients' self-reflection, to generating patient-friendly clinical notes that help patients actively participate in their own emergency care.

MindfulDiary · CHI '24 DiaryMate · CHI '24 Clinical Notes · Sci Rep '26

Digital Mental Health

Innovative approaches to improving mental well-being through technology. MindScope monitors stress from smartphone activities, while a social bot design explores care-receiver interactions and stigma reduction.

MindScope · CHI '22 Gloomy · CHI '20
