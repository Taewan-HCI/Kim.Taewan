HCI Research Scientist · TwelveLabs

Taewan Kim, Ph.D.

HCI researcher and designer working at the intersection of human-AI interaction and AI-driven service design. Currently an HCI Research Scientist at TwelveLabs, with prior industry experience at Samsung Electronics and NAVER AI Lab. I earned my Ph.D. from KAIST under the guidance of Professor Hwajung Hong, where my research explored how large language models and multimodal AI can be meaningfully applied to real-world services in healthcare and mental wellbeing, with a focus on bridging academic research and real-world practice.

Research Vision & Framing

Designing AI as an Interactive Layer of Human Interpretation

AI is becoming the layer through which people perceive, navigate, and act in the world. It is no longer just generating static information, but increasingly shaping human interpretation through interaction.

AI mediates how humans understand themselves.

Exploring how AI can support mental well-being through everyday interaction and reflection.
